<h2 id="our-principles">Our Principles</h2>

<p>
  Civic Tech DC projects are guided by a few principles:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>
    <strong>Problem-first:</strong> Our projects start with a real problem rather than a technology looking for somewhere to be used. We talk to the people experiencing the problem and understand what they need before deciding what to build.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Time-boxed:</strong> Volunteer time is limited. Our projects work toward clear goals and milestones rather than remaining open-ended indefinitely.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Sustainable:</strong> Our projects are designed so that the work can continue even when the people who started it move on. From the beginning, we emphasize documentation, shared ownership, and a plan for transition.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Open by default:</strong> Open source and open work makes it easier for other contributors and communities to learn from and build on what you have done.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Minimal bureaucracy:</strong> We value structure where structure is needed. This allows projects to stay nimble and keep moving while not feeling disorganized.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Protect volunteer capacity:</strong> Our projects respect people's time and avoid creating expectations that volunteers cannot realistically maintain.
  </li>
</ul>
